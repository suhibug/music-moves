Music Moves

We've always moved to the music. What if the music moved to us?

  • Objects float in the air around you, picked to match where you are
  • Grab, stretch, throw, catch, bat, smash. Every move makes a sound
  • Keep playing and a beat grows underneath. Stop, and it goes quiet
